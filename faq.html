Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from Sindo Wellness.

Ordering & Payment

How do I pay?

Manual bank transfer. After you place an order, we email you our bank account details (BCA, QRIS-compatible, GoPay, or Mandiri VA). You transfer the exact total, then send us the payment proof via WhatsApp. Your tracking number is issued automatically once we confirm receipt.

Can I cancel or change my order after paying?

You can cancel any time before we place the supplier order (typically within 24 hours of payment). After that, we cannot cancel because the items have been ordered and the shipping pipeline is in motion.

Are the products 100% original?

Yes. We order directly from our authorized supplier and ship the same sealed bottles you would receive from them directly. Every product detail page links to its source listing for full transparency.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?

After your payment is confirmed (usually within 24 hours), our team places the order and items are consolidated at our Yishun hub. Total delivery time is 5-10 business days worldwide, depending on your address.

How is the shipping fee calculated?

S$3 per 100g of total cart weight, plus a 10% markup on the final total. Per-item breakdown is shown in your cart before checkout.

Are customs duties included?

Yes, for shipments up to 1.5 kg per order. Heavier orders will require a separate quote — contact us via WhatsApp before ordering.

When will I get my tracking number?

Your tracking number is issued automatically the moment your payment is confirmed. You'll see it on the order confirmation page and in our follow-up WhatsApp message.

Do you ship outside Indonesia?

Yes. While our warehouses are based in Singapore and Indonesia, we can ship to most countries worldwide via Sindo Shipping's partner network. Contact us first for a quote.

Returns & Refunds

What if my product arrives damaged or wrong?

We replace or refund any item that arrives damaged, opened, or different from what you ordered. See our Returns policy for full details.

Privacy

How is my data handled?

We only collect the data needed to fulfill your order (name, email, phone, shipping address). We never sell or share it. See our Privacy policy for full details.
