About Sindo Wellness

Sindo Wellness is the customer-facing storefront of Sindo Shipping, a Singapore-based logistics company that specialises in two-way shipments between Singapore and Indonesia.

What we do

We curate a focused catalog of authentic supplements — vitamins, minerals, fish oils, probiotics, prenatal care, and a few specialty items — and we ship them from Singapore to your door via our own logistics network. After your payment is confirmed, the Sindo team orders your items from our partner US warehouse, consolidates them at our Yishun hub, and ships them to your address through our Batam facility and local last-mile couriers.

Why our supplier?

Our upstream supplier is one of the world's largest natural-products retailers, with rigorous authenticity standards, climate-controlled warehousing, and direct relationships with brands like NOW Foods, California Gold Nutrition, Thorne, Life Extension, and Garden of Life. We don't repackage or relabel — what arrives at your door is the exact same sealed product our supplier ships, delivered via Sindo's Singapore-based logistics network.

Why Sindo Shipping?

Most cross-border supplement shipments rely on multiple handoffs between commercial carriers. Each handoff is a customs risk, a delay risk, and a tracking-loss risk. Sindo Shipping operates the entire pipeline from Yishun (Singapore) → Batam (Indonesia) → your local address, with one tracking number and one team responsible for end-to-end delivery.

Pricing

All product prices are listed in SGD, sourced live from our supplier's catalog. The price you see includes the source cost, our 10% platform markup, and flat S$3 per 100g shipping via Sindo Shipping. No hidden fees.

  • Shipping: S$3 per 100g of total weight, plus 10% platform markup
  • Customs duties: Included for shipments up to 1.5 kg per order (above this, contact us for a quote)
  • Payment: Manual bank transfer (BCA, QRIS, GoPay, Mandiri VA)

What's next

We are a single tenant on the Katalog.id multi-tenant e-commerce platform. Once we hit our volume targets, we plan to add more catalog categories (personal care, groceries, fitness nutrition) and open the platform to other Indonesian tenant stores.
