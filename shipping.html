Delivery time

  • Indonesia: 5-7 business days after payment confirmation
  • Singapore: 2-3 business days after payment confirmation
  • Malaysia, Brunei, Thailand: 7-10 business days
  • Rest of world: 10-15 business days via partner network

How it works (5-stage tracking)

  1. iHerb manually ordered — Once your payment is confirmed, our team places the order on iHerb.com within 24 hours.
  2. SG Yishun hub — Items arrive at our Yishun consolidation warehouse (Singapore) within 3-5 days from iHerb's US warehouse.
  3. ID Batam warehouse — Consolidated shipment is forwarded to our Batam facility (Indonesia).
  4. Local last mile — Your order is handed to a local courier (JNE, J&T, SiCepat) for delivery.
  5. Delivered — Courier delivers to your address. You receive a WhatsApp notification.

Each stage is visible at track.html. Your tracking number format is SS-YYYYMM-XXXXX.

Customs & duties

For shipments up to 1.5 kg, customs duties for Indonesia are included in the shipping fee. For shipments over 1.5 kg or to other countries, we will quote duties separately before shipping.

Restricted items

We do not ship flammable, pressurised, or controlled substances. All products in our catalog are pre-checked for cross-border shipment eligibility. If a product is restricted in your destination country, we will notify you before payment.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package is lost in transit or arrives damaged, we replace or refund at no charge. See our Returns policy for the claim process.
