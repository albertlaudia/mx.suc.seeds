K-Beauty FAQ

What does "routine-based curation" mean?

Every product on this storefront fits a real step in a Korean skincare routine — cleanser, toner, essence, serum, moisturizer, SPF, treatment, or lip care. No fillers, no "glow kits" packed with impulse buys.

Are these original K-beauty products?

Yes. All items are sourced from authorized Korean distributors and shipped from Singapore via Sindo Shipping. Authenticity guaranteed or full refund.

How do I build a routine?

Start with a cleanser (Anua or COSRX), follow with a toner/essence (COSRX Snail 96, Some By Mi), layer a serum (Beauty of Joseon Glow or SKIN1004 Centella), lock in with a moisturizer (Laneige Water Bank or Innisfree Green Tea), and finish with SPF (Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun) in the morning. Add a weekly mask (COSRX Pimple Patch or TIRTIR Cushion for events) as needed.

Are these brands cruelty-free?

Yes — every brand on this storefront is cruelty-free. Most are also vegan-friendly. COSRX, Anua, Beauty of Joseon, SKIN1004, Some By Mi, and Innisfree are all certified.

How long does shipping take?

Sindo Shipping delivers to most destinations in 5–10 days from Singapore. You will receive a tracking number as soon as your bank-transfer payment is confirmed.

What if a product irritates my skin?

Always patch-test new products on the inner forearm or behind the ear before full-face use. Discontinue if irritation occurs. WhatsApp customer service daily for routine troubleshooting.

What payment methods do you accept?

Manual bank transfer (BCA · QRIS · GoPay · Mandiri VA) is supported today. Stripe Elements and GrabPay/ShopeePay are planned for Q4.

Can I return opened products?

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. For hygiene reasons, opened cosmetics are not returnable unless defective. WhatsApp us if you receive a damaged item.
