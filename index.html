Quick answers

How long does shipping take?

5–10 days worldwide. Once payment is confirmed, your order is placed with our partner US warehouse the same day. We consolidate in Singapore (3–5 days), clear Indonesia customs at our Batam hub (1–2 days), and dispatch last-mile via Sindo Shipping (1–3 days).

Are the products really authentic?

Yes. Every product is sourced from our authorized US distributor (sg.iherb.com). Each product page links directly to its source listing for verification. If anything we sell turns out not to be authentic, we refund 100% — no questions.

How do I pay?

Manual bank transfer (BCA, QRIS, GoPay, Mandiri VA, DBS) today. Stripe card payments coming Q4 2026. After you place an order, we send the bank details via WhatsApp within 5 minutes. You transfer, then send us a screenshot. Your tracking number is issued as soon as we confirm.

What's the shipping fee?

Flat S$3 per 100g, with a 10% platform markup. Customs duties are included up to 1.5kg. Free shipping on orders over S$80. See the shipping policy for the full pricing table.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship to Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei, Thailand, and worldwide via Sindo Shipping. Most destinations take 5–10 days. Customs handling included for ASEAN destinations.

Where is the catalog sourced from?

11 products today, sourced via Bright Data snapshots from sg.iherb.com. We manually verify every product before it goes live, including a side-by-side price comparison, image sanity check, and source link validation. Catalog is refreshed weekly.
