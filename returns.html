Returns & Refunds

We stand behind every product. If something is wrong, we make it right.

What we cover

  • Damaged items — Bottle cracked, seal broken, packaging crushed in transit.
  • Wrong item shipped — Different product or size than what you ordered.
  • Counterfeit concern — If you suspect the item is not authentic, send us a photo of the iHerb-sealed packaging and we'll refund no questions asked.
  • Lost in transit — If the tracking number shows no movement for 14+ days, we replace or refund.

What we don't cover

  • Change of mind — Supplements are consumables; once ordered from iHerb, we cannot return them.
  • Adverse reactions — If a product disagrees with you, that's a medical matter, not a refund matter. Always consult your doctor.
  • Delays caused by incorrect address — Re-shipments due to wrong address are at customer's cost.

How to file a claim

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to our CS line (see Contact) within 7 days of receiving the item.
  2. Include your order number and photos of the item + packaging.
  3. We respond within 24 hours with a resolution (refund, replacement, or both).

Refund timing

Refunds are processed back to the same bank account / e-wallet you used for the original payment, within 3-5 business days of approval.

Authenticity guarantee

Every product page on our store links directly to its source listing on iHerb.com. If the item you receive does not match the iHerb listing (wrong brand, wrong formulation, wrong country of origin), you are entitled to a full refund including original shipping. We do not require you to return the item in this case — keep it or dispose of it.
