Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of how we handle your data. Last updated: 2026-09-23.

What we collect

Only the data we need to fulfill your order:

  • Name — for shipping label and customs forms
  • Email — for order confirmation and tracking updates
  • Phone — for the courier to reach you
  • Shipping address — for delivery
  • Order history — linked to your order ref

We do not collect: date of birth, government ID, payment card numbers (we use bank transfer only), or any sensitive personal data.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your order
  • To send you order-related communications (confirmation, tracking, delivery)
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To comply with tax and customs record-keeping requirements

We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit opt-in.

Who we share it with

  • Sindo Shipping — to deliver your package (name, phone, address only)
  • iHerb — your order data flows to iHerb only when our team manually orders on your behalf (name, address, item list). iHerb does not see your payment information.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you message us on WhatsApp, Meta processes that conversation under their own privacy policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third-party marketer, analytics provider, or advertising network.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: 5 years (required by Indonesian tax law)
  • Customer service conversations: 2 years
  • Marketing opt-ins: until you unsubscribe

Your rights

  • Access — ask what data we hold on you
  • Correction — ask us to fix wrong data
  • Deletion — ask us to delete data that is no longer needed for tax/legal reasons
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from any non-essential communication

To exercise any of these, send a WhatsApp message to our CS line (see Contact) with your order number. We respond within 7 days.

Cookies

We use only functional cookies (cart state, language preference). No advertising cookies, no third-party tracking, no Google Analytics on this storefront at the moment.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify active customers by email and post a notice on the home page for 30 days before the change takes effect.
